# Image generation record

All 11 source PNGs were generated separately using the built-in `image_gen` tool, with opaque photographic backgrounds. No reference photograph was recovered or claimed as an original. Source resolution and production variants are in the asset manifest. The following briefs record the generation intent and composition used for each call.

1. **hero-sigiriya** — Premium realistic Sri Lankan travel panorama, 2:1. Sigiriya fortress in the right half above dense jungle; ancient summit ruins, layered misty mountains, peach-gold sunrise at upper right. Pale atmospheric landscape at left for the CSS white fade. Natural geology and forest-green/amber palette. No text, logos, UI or watermark.
2. **elephant** — Portrait 4:5. Single Asian elephant walking toward the camera in a three-quarter frontal view, head, trunk and front legs visible. Small ears, earthy wrinkled skin, lush tropical forest and foreground foliage, soft warm morning light. Respectful wildlife observation; no handlers, riding or collars. No text/watermark.
3. **hill-country** — Landscape 4:3. Rolling emerald tea terraces, winding foreground path, small tea-estate houses, distant layered blue mountains and luminous peach morning sky. Elevated view across a cultivated valley. Natural detail; no text/logos.
4. **blue-train** — Landscape 5:3. Blue passenger train on Ella's curved stone Nine Arch Bridge, viewed diagonally from above, arches visible below, surrounding jungle and tea hills, blue mountain layers and golden morning light. No text/logos. An illustrative recreation, not a verified railway record.
5. **southern-coast** — Landscape 5:3. Coconut palms leaning from the left across the top, sunset low over the Indian Ocean at right, pale sand, shoreline rocks, gentle waves and peach clouds. Deep green and gold palette; no people, text or logos.
6. **reservoir-travellers** — Landscape 4:3. Two adult travellers seen from behind on a rocky viewpoint: man with olive backpack left, woman with straw hat and light backpack right. Winding turquoise reservoir, lush green mountains and distant misty ridges, expansive warm morning landscape. No text/logos.
7. **tea-pickers** — Horizontal 2:1. Two Sri Lankan women working among tea bushes. Main woman right of center, coral blouse, teal apron, burgundy headscarf and basket, looking down at leaves with a natural smile. Second woman in muted blue behind left. Warm daylight, soft palms/hills background. No text/logos. These are generated people, not photographs of the business team.
8. **culture** — Portrait 3:5. Weathered red-brick temple ruins, carved stone standing Buddha, steps, pillars, tropical trees and pale blue sky. Polonnaruwa-inspired cultural composition, warm morning light, respectful travel photography. No visitors/text/logos. Not a documentary site record.
9. **beaches** — Portrait 3:5. Coconut palms leaning from left over turquoise sea, pale sand, rocks and transparent water. Palm fronds frame clear sky; rich natural daylight. No people/text/logos.
10. **sri-lankan-food** — Portrait 3:5. Rice and curry in terracotta bowls on dark wood, dhal, vegetable curry, sambol, papadum, curry leaves and spices. Warm side light and shallow depth of field. No writing/logos.
11. **tropical-cta** — Panorama 3:1. Crescent turquoise cove, golden sand, granite boulders, dense palms and coastal forest; elevated view diagonally along the coast, sea through center and green shore at upper right. Bright natural sun. No people/buildings/text/logos.

Three additional scene assets are crops: cultural-triangle from hero-sigiriya, wildlife from elephant, and tea-country from hill-country. Decorations and the brand mark are separate authored SVGs; notes and handwritten text remain independently positioned rather than flattened into photography.
