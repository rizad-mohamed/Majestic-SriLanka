# SEO scope and search intent

Research date: 31 October 2026. A small live search sample for private Sri Lanka tours, tailor-made holidays, Cultural Triangle and hill-country journeys was reviewed. This is qualitative intent research, not keyword-volume or ranking data.

Public operator pages organize these themes into routes, personalised planning and experience categories: [Hill City Tours](https://www.hillcitytours.com/tours/), [Tamarind Holiday](https://tamarindholiday.com/) and [Ceylonara tours](https://ceylonara.lk/tours/). The inference is that visitors need both suggested itineraries and a clear way to personalise a journey. No competitor copy, prices, credentials, customer claims or contact details are imported into Majestic Sri Lanka.

| Primary route | Main intent | Relevant topic language |
|---|---|---|
| Home | Understand the brand and begin exploring | Sri Lanka private tours, local journeys |
| Tours | Compare suggested routes | Cultural Triangle itinerary, hill-country journey, southern coast |
| Tailor-Made | Learn how a personal holiday is shaped | Tailor-made Sri Lanka holidays, personalised itinerary |
| Experiences | Explore by interest | Wildlife, culture, beaches, tea country, Sri Lankan food |
| About Us | Assess approach and trust | Majestic Sri Lanka story, local expertise, responsible travel |
| Plan Your Trip | Convert an idea into a planning brief | Sri Lanka trip planner, personal travel brief |
| Contact Us | Find verified business details and answers | Majestic Sri Lanka contact, travel FAQ |

Distinct titles and descriptions are implemented for every route. Body copy stays useful and editorial rather than repeating keywords. Headings, descriptive alt text, internal links, canonical URLs, Open Graph/Twitter metadata, WebPage/WebSite JSON-LD, a seven-entry sitemap and matching robots directives are generated in the static output.

The private review build is intentionally noindex. `majesticsrilanka.com` and `info@majesticsrilanka.com` are proposed placeholders, not verified live services. Before public indexing, confirm the domain and contacts, set `PUBLIC_SITE_URL` to the final HTTPS origin, approve business/legal content, then enable `PUBLIC_ALLOW_INDEXING` and rebuild. No fabricated review, offer, price, address or certification schema is included.

After public launch, verify ownership in the chosen search-console service, submit the sitemap, check indexing/canonical selection and review actual query and conversion data. Field Core Web Vitals require real traffic; Lighthouse scores do not establish real-user INP or search rankings.
