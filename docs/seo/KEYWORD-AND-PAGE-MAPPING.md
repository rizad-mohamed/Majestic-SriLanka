Homepage owns broad service discovery; Tours owns itinerary comparison; Tailor-Made owns custom planning explanation; Planner owns brief creation. Their distinct content and titles avoid assigning the same exact intent to every route. Shared navigation links all seven; added contextual links support research without changing page architecture.

Related questions are already covered in the contact FAQ: personalising journeys, starting a brief, outline versus confirmed proposal, dietary/accessibility requests and railway availability. Do not add speculative destination/visa facts. English is the only actual language (`lang=en`); no hreflang or regional duplicates are appropriate. Google Business Profile/LocalBusiness eligibility requires verified business operations and address/service-area evidence. No profile, citation, outreach or ownership operation was performed.

Future content should use client-approved routes, inclusion details, authentic first-hand destination material and licensed original photography. Measure real query intent after Search Console setup; revisit the map using actual impressions and enquiry quality, never keyword density.
